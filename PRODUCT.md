# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective early adopters, technical partners, and teams evaluating a new robotic arm. The exact industry and operating environment are still undecided.

## Product Purpose

ROSE is a robotic-arm concept. The website is a full flagship product and brand experience that explains the concept, presents its design direction, outlines planned capabilities, and lets interested visitors join a waitlist for updates.

## Positioning

Open decision. No specific capability, performance advantage, industry specialization, or technical differentiator has been confirmed yet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed product type: robotic arm.
- Primary website action: join the waitlist.
- The site must not present invented specifications, benchmarks, customers, certifications, pricing, or shipping dates as facts.
- Concept renders, interface demonstrations, and product details may be authored when they are clearly labeled as placeholders.
- The website should support light and dark modes and use purposeful 3D parallax scroll motion.

## Brand Commitments

- Confirmed working brand name: ROSE.
- Visual direction: simplistic, professional, easy to read, and Apple-adjacent in restraint and product presentation.
- Mostly white or cool cream surfaces with one reddish-pink accent.
- Dark mode must preserve the same hierarchy and brand character.

## Evidence on Hand

No logo, product photography, CAD model, 3D model, technical specifications, customer proof, or other production assets are currently available. All visual product material created during this phase is placeholder concept material.

## Product Principles

- Make the product understandable within the first viewport.
- Keep the waitlist action obvious without turning the page into a sales funnel.
- Use motion to explain form and movement, not as decoration.
- Separate concept material from confirmed product truth.
- Keep the interface calm, legible, and accessible in both color modes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site should support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
